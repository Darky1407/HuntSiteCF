Before it begins, understand this:

  • It has no interest in what a machine can solve for you. If you bring one, bring it only when you have truly stopped.
  • Download what it gives you. Some things must leave this page before they can be understood.
  • You will require tools it has not provided. You will know them when you need them.
  • It was not built for small screens. Sit where you can see everything.
  • Some of what it says, it does not say in words. Keep your sound on.
  • You will never need to leave where you are sitting.
  • What you do is remembered. Leave if you must — it will still know you when you return.
  • The name you give it must be plain. No capitals. No spaces.
  • There is no clock here. It has already waited longer than you can.
